How do I send you the raw video clips?

You can simply upload your phone or camera videos to a Google Drive folder and send me the link on WhatsApp. If clips are small, you can even share them directly on WhatsApp document or Telegram!

Can you add subtitles in Gujarati or Hindi?

Yes, absolutely! I specialize in dynamic, stylized captions in Gujarati, Hindi, and English so your local audience understands every single word clearly.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

Because time and creative work are invested immediately upon starting, I do not offer refunds. However, every project comes with 1 free revision round to adjust music, pacing, captions, or cuts until you are completely satisfied with the final video.

How fast do you deliver?

Standard delivery is 24 to 48 hours for individual reels. For monthly package clients, we set up a smooth calendar schedule so your Instagram is never dry.
